About

From enterprise phone rooms to Main Street.

My career started in telecom, and it never really left. For more than two decades I worked on the systems large organizations depend on to talk to the public: phone systems, the connections to the phone carriers, and the routing that gets each call to the right person.

Along the way I saw the same problem in businesses of every size: calls missed, messages lost, follow-up that depends on someone remembering. Small businesses feel it most, because one missed call can be the job that pays the week.

So I started CallDirector AI to give local service businesses what the big organizations have: every call answered, every lead followed up, and a clear record of what happened. I live in Germantown, Maryland, and work with businesses across the DMV and beyond.
